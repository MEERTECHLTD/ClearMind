    Getting started

    From first visit to a clear day in minutes.

    1. 1

      Open ClearMind

      Use it in your browser or install it from Google Play. Start as a guest straight away, or create an account with email, Google or GitHub on the web.

    2. 2

      Get it out of your head

      Drop tasks into Quick Add and ideas into Notes. Sort them into projects later — or as you type with #project.

    3. 3

      Plan the day

      Work from Today, look ahead in Upcoming, and ask Iris to break big tasks into steps you can start.

    4. 4

      Stay in sync

      Sign in and your workspace syncs across your devices, so the list on your phone matches the one on your laptop.

    Privacy

    Your workspace is yours.

    The commitments below come straight from our privacy policy.

    • No ads, no tracking SDKs

      ClearMind contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We don’t sell your information.

    • Keep it local if you like

      The web app’s Local Workspace keeps your data in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

    • Encrypted in transit and at rest

      Synced data is stored with Google Firebase, encrypted over HTTPS/TLS in transit and encrypted at rest by Google.

    • AI only when you ask

      Text is sent to Google’s Gemini API only when you use an AI feature, to generate that response.

    • Agents on your terms

      Connected AI agents get only the permissions you grant. Their actions are logged and you can revoke access at any time.

    • Leave whenever you want

      Export your data from Settings on the web, and delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Something else? Email secure@meertech.tech or visit Support.

    Who is ClearMind for?

    Anyone who keeps their work and life in more places than they’d like — professionals, developers, students and creators who want tasks, notes and plans in one place.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You can start as a guest. On the web, a Local Workspace keeps everything in your browser on that device. Create an account when you want your workspace to sync across devices and to use the AI features.

    Does ClearMind cost anything?

    The current web and Android apps have no paid plans, subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

    Which devices does it work on?

    Any modern browser, and Android through Google Play. An iPhone app is in development and not yet on the App Store.

    How does the AI assistant use my data?

    When you use an AI feature such as Iris or the application reviewer, the text you submit for that request is sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate the answer. Nothing is sent to it otherwise. Please avoid submitting sensitive information to AI features.

    Can I use ClearMind with Claude or ChatGPT?

    Yes. ClearMind works as a connector for Claude and ChatGPT, and as an MCP server, REST API or CLI for other agents. You choose each connection’s permissions in Settings and can revoke it at any time.

    How do I delete my account?

    From inside the app — this removes your content, files, profile and agent tokens. Step-by-step instructions are on the account deletion page.
